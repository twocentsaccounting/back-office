Step 2 of 4

Export Your Trial Balance from QBO

Follow these steps inside QuickBooks Online to get your data ready to paste:

  1. Log in to QuickBooks Online and click Reports in the left sidebar.
  2. In the search box, type "Trial Balance" and click the report when it appears.
  3. Set your date range — typically the most recent full month or your current fiscal year to date.
  4. Click Run Report to generate the data.
  5. Click the Export icon (top right of the report) and choose Export to Excel.
  6. Open the downloaded .xlsx file in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.
  7. Press Ctrl+A (or Cmd+A on Mac) to select all cells, then Ctrl+C (Cmd+C) to copy.
  8. Head to Step 4 and paste the data into the text area.
